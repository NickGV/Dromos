# Area habits

Objective: an `archeion` note can declare `habits` like a campaign `index`, and the Library's reading room shows the reading area's habits beside the desk, with their streak and "Send to Habitica".
Branch: `feat/area-habits` · Delivery: `ask-on-risk` · Checks: `npm test` (Vitest), `npm run lint`, `npm run build`, then the user's look in Obsidian.

## Specs

- **S1** — The request, relayed by Esdras (2026-11-09): "Propuesta de Esdras para Dromos, a pedido de Nick: que una nota `archeion` pueda declarar `habits`, igual que un `index`. Nick pregunta si se puede hacer ahora." The proposal: "`habits: [{title, days, skill, habitica_id}]` también en `type: archeion`, con el mismo esquema y la misma regla de escritura (el plugin solo escribe `habitica_id` al pulsar "Enviar a Habitica"). Así el hábito vive en `03 - Areas/Bibliotheke/Archeion - Bibliotheke.md`, cuya `skill` ya es `lector`."
- **S2** — Where they show. The user chose "En la Biblioteca (Recomendado)", the option that read: "La sala muestra los hábitos de su Archeion junto a la mesa de lectura, con la racha y "Enviar a Habitica". Es lo más cerca de donde lees. Contra: los hábitos de un área sin pantalla propia (Kairos, Cheshbon) no se verían en ningún lado hasta que tengan una."
- **S3** — The contract (ADR-017 in the vault). Same schema, defaults and rules as campaign habits (`days` 21 by default, strict streak: one missed day resets it). Dromos writes only `habitica_id`, only inside `habits`, only when the user selects "Enviar a Habitica". Which habits the Library shows (rewritten by L4): every habit of an `archeion` note that is not `archived` whose effective skills include `lector`, the skill of the reading to-do (ADR-016, Q027); its effective skills are its own `skill`, or else its Archeion's. The user chose "Por la skill del hábito (Recomendado)", the option that read: "Entra un hábito cuya skill es lector, o que no tiene skill y su Archeion sí la tiene. "Leer 10 minutos" (lector) entra; un hábito de Learnings con skill estudiante no. Un hábito de lectura de otra área (por ejemplo Bible) también entraría."
- **S4** — The streak comes from the same sync as a campaign habit's: when the view opens and on **Sync**, never on a timer (Habitica allows 30 requests per minute).
- **S5** — Migration, done by Esdras after the release: the `habits` block that Esdras put in `02 - Projects/Dromos/README.md` as a stopgap moves to `Archeion - Bibliotheke` with its `habitica_id`, so the streak is kept (it lives in the Habitica daily). Esdras also adds `habits` to the `archeion` row of the dictionary in `logos-dotfiles`. Out of this repository's scope.
