# M10 · La tienda: rewards, Esdras's offers and the level bonus

Objective: a Shop screen where the user buys, with his Habitica gold, his own Habitica rewards and the offers Esdras writes, and a gold bonus paid once for each new level. Contract: vault `02 - Projects/Dromos/03-Tech-Decisions.md`, ADR-019; plan: `02-Phases.md`, M10.
Branch `feat/m10-shop`. Delivery: work-unit commits, `--no-ff` merge into `main` and release only with the user's go-ahead. Runner: Vitest (`npm test`), then `npm run lint` and `npm run build`.

## Specs

The user's idea (L1): "estaba pensando en realizar algo asi como una tienda donde estarian algunas recompensas que vienen directamente de habitica y que yo pueda comprar y tambien quizas comprar y podria ser tambien que esdras me venda algunas". His changes (L2): "con el tema del 3 no creo que sea por nivel, terminaria con muchos titulos" and "creo que mejor el oro aparezca solo en la tienda, no quiero verlo en la placa del personaje, el oro viene y debe venir directamente de habitica, si se puede el tema del oro extra podria estar bien, porque nos ayudaria con el tema de la recompensas". His approval (L4): "si asi me parece bien", to Claude's plan, whose key point was: "que Dromos toque tu oro en Habitica en dos casos: restar cuando comprás, siempre con confirmación, y sumar el bono cuando subís de nivel".

- **S1 The gold and the Habitica rewards.** "recompensas que vienen directamente de habitica y que yo pueda comprar"; "el oro viene y debe venir directamente de habitica".
  - The gold is Habitica's `stats.gp`; Dromos keeps no currency of its own and shows the gold only in the Shop (never on the plaque), as a whole number.
  - The Shop lists the user's own Habitica rewards (tasks of type `reward`): their name, their notes when they have any, and their price (`value`). Rewards carrying the Habitica tag `kairos` are not listed (L13): Kairos buys them itself, and buying one elsewhere spends the gold with no minutes in Kairos.
  - Buying one asks for a confirmation that names the reward, its price and the gold left after it; confirmed, it buys it in Habitica (`POST /api/v3/tasks/:id/score/up`, which takes the gold) and shows the new gold Habitica returns. A reward that costs more than the gold there is cannot be bought, and says why.
  - The Shop reads the gold and the rewards when it opens (and on Sync), never on a timer (Habitica allows 30 requests a minute). Offline or without credentials, it says so and lets nothing be bought. A failed purchase says what happened and changes nothing in the vault.
- **S2 Esdras's offers.** "podria ser tambien que esdras me venda algunas". Rewritten after Esdras's review (L5).
  - Offers live in `<Dromos folder>/Shop.md` (`type: dromos-shop`), a table `| id | offer | price | why | until |` that only Esdras writes (ADR-019). Dromos only reads it, by its path, even when its `type` is missing.
  - Parsing: the first table with that header; columns matched by header name, not position; cells trimmed; the `|---|` row and alignment colons ignored (the Table Editor plugin re-pads tables). `id` is `[a-z0-9-]`; `price` a positive integer; `until` a `YYYY-MM-DD` date (the last day it is offered, inclusive, by local date) or empty. A row with a bad `id`, `price` or `until` is skipped and reported; with a repeated `id` the first row counts; the other rows still show.
  - The Shop shows the open offers (not bought, `until` not past) with their offer, why, price and "until" date. Expired and bought rows are only hidden.
  - Buying one, after the same confirmation as S1: Dromos looks up the Habitica task with alias `dromos-offer-<id>` and creates the reward with that alias only when it does not exist (a retry never creates a second one), buys it, writes the purchase into the purse (S3, with the offer's `id`), and deletes that reward from Habitica. Rewards whose alias starts with `dromos-offer-` are never listed as the user's own (S1), even when the delete failed. A bought offer (an `id` in the purse) is not offered again.
- **S3 The level bonus and the purse.** "si se puede el tema del oro extra podria estar bien, porque nos ayudaria con el tema de la recompensas"; and the plan's "Dromos te suma oro en Habitica cada vez que subís de nivel, por ejemplo 10 por cada nivel nuevo (30 al pasar al 3). El monto queda en la nota de reglas del vault, así lo podés cambiar. Dromos anota qué niveles ya pagó, así nunca te paga dos veces el mismo."
  - When the game sync finds the character reached a new level, Dromos adds `gold_per_level × that level` gold in Habitica (`PUT /api/v3/user` setting `stats.gp` to the gold it just read plus the bonus), once for each level reached (two levels at once pay both). `gold_per_level` comes from the rules note (`Rules.md`) and is 10 when the note does not set it. No bonus for levels reached before M10 ships.
  - The purse, `<Dromos folder>/Purse.md` (`type: dromos-purse`), a table `| date | kind | what | gold | level | offer |` that only Dromos writes: one row for each purchase (`purchase`, negative gold, with the offer's `id` for an offer) and each bonus (`bonus`, with its level), written when Habitica confirms. Before paying a bonus Dromos reads the purse again, and a level with a `bonus` row is never paid again. A failed payment writes nothing and is tried again on the next sync. A payment whose outcome is unknown (no answer, a timeout: Habitica may still apply it) keeps the gold read before it and the gold it asked for; the next sync reads the gold again first: the gold asked for means it was paid (its purse row is written, nothing is sent), the gold before means it was not (paid again from the fresh gold), and anything else is never paid again and the user is told to check their gold for that level (L8).
  - The user hears of the bonus ("+30 gold for reaching level 3") as Dromos announces other game news.
- **S4 The Shop screen.** "una tienda"; and the plan's "una tabla nueva en el cartel, Tienda".
  - A sixth plank, **Shop** (Spanish **Tienda**), after Library and before Settings; the signpost hangs three planks a side; the current one burnt in, as today. The Shop opens with its header card ("Shop"), the gold, the Habitica rewards, Esdras's offers, and the last purchases from the purse.
  - Copy in English and Spanish; light and dark themes; readable at 360 px; every button and amount named in words for screen readers.
- **S6 The stall.** "me lo imaginaba mas asi, como si yo estuviera dentro del puesto y claramente todo anbientado en aquella epoca" (L10), with a reference image (`/home/ngomez/Pictures/Screenshots/Pasted image.png`): a first-person view at a merchant's stall, the merchant behind the counter, shelves of wares, hanging lamps, a grid of item tiles on the left (each with its drawing and price, locked when out of reach), the chosen item's detail on the right (its drawing, name, description and a buy button), the coins shown at the top.
  - The Shop becomes a stall of the Dromos world's era (biblical and Greco-Roman antiquity, as the papyrus library, the clay lamps and the pilgrim are): a merchant in tunic and mantle behind a wooden counter under a striped linen awning; shelves of clay amphorae, oil jars, baskets of figs and dates, rolled scrolls; a balance scale, a pile of gold coins and a leather pouch on the counter; clay oil lamps, never glass lanterns; the market and the sky behind, painted by the hour and the theme with the scene's tokens. Same line style as the landscape and the library.
  - The wares as tiles: each reward or offer a tile with a drawn object, its name and its price; a ware the gold cannot reach shows a lock and how much is missing. The object comes from a small drawn set by price (cheaper to dearer, for example a basket of figs, an oil lamp, a scroll, an amphora, a cloth of purple, a small chest), since Habitica rewards carry no picture. Tabs over the tiles: the user's Habitica rewards, Esdras's offers, and the last purchases.
  - Choosing a tile shows it in a detail panel: its drawing, its name, its notes or Esdras's why, its price and the gold left after it, and **Buy**, which opens the existing confirmation (S1, S3 unchanged: nothing is bought without it). The gold sits on a coin plaque at the top.
  - On a phone the stall shrinks to a band with the merchant, the tiles follow in a grid, and the detail opens below the tiles. Light and dark themes; every tile a button named by its ware, price and whether it can be bought; reduced motion respected; the merchant may breathe or gesture in a calm loop like the landscape's life.
- **S5 Release.** Publish the version and check it on desktop and Android, with the user's go-ahead.
