# M7: Library (Biblioteca)

Objective: a papyrus library of the vault's books, by shelf and by state, where a book is sealed only once its reflection is written. The vault contracts come first (V tasks), then the plugin (U tasks).
Branch: vault notes are not in git; `logos-dotfiles` changes stay uncommitted until the user says; plugin work on `feat/m7-library` · Delivery: `ask-on-risk` (forecast about 1,500 authored lines in the plugin, so the chain strategy is asked before the first plugin commit) · Runner: `npm test`, `npm run lint`, `npm run build` for the plugin; the vault normalizer dry-run for the vault.

## Specs

Decisions live in the vault: `02 - Projects/Dromos/05-Open-Questions.md` (Q024 to Q028), `02-Phases.md` (M7) and `08-Visual-Design.md` (Biblioteca).

- **S1** — The book note (Q024, Q025). "yo creo que un libro no debe estar en resources, puede ser mejor un area nombrada con algo representativo, porque no me suena que sea un resource"; on the proposal: "sisi, vamos con lo que tu dices, es perfecto el nombre"; on the shelf: "si me suena el campo shelf para saber donde ponerlo". Books live in `03 - Areas/Bibliotheke/`, with an `Archeion - Bibliotheke` (goal: leer con intención; standard: cada libro terminado tiene su reflexión; skill: `lector`). `type: book` is a new work-family type (ADR-016 amends ADR-013 to 18 types). State is the lifecycle `status`: `planning` = pendiente, `active` = leyendo, `paused` = en pausa, `completed` = terminado. Optional dates `started` and `finished`; `shelf` holds one shelf, written as shown on screen ("Teología"); a book without one goes to "Sin estante".
- **S2** — The reflection (Q026): "si el rollo se sella cuando estas tienen texto". Four sections: `## De qué trata`, `## Qué me quedó`, `## Qué cambia en mí`, `## Con qué lo conecto`. Finished = `status: completed` and all four sections have text; otherwise the book shows "Falta la reflexión", not sealed. Dromos reads it through the metadata cache (headings and sections).
- **S3** — The reading to-do (Q027): "deberiamos hacer que se cree la tarea de lectura una vez pase el libro a reading, para que empiece a contar desde alli"; "si pienso que el todo debe tener la reflexion, me parece correcto la verdad"; "con esto yo me comprometo a no realizarlo, a no completarla sin hacer la rreflexion". "Empezar a leer" creates one Habitica to-do with skill `lector` and a checklist ("Terminar de leer" plus the four reflection questions), and stores its `habitica_id` in the note.
- **S4** — Who writes (Q028): "yo le digo a el que libros quiero leer y el me crea las notas donde deberian estar y me gustaria que desde dromos pueda cambiar el estado". Esdras creates the note from `Templates/book.md` in `03 - Areas/Bibliotheke/`, with `status: planning` and no to-do. A book the user already finished: "agrega como primer libro "Klara y el Sol" El cual ya lo termine Porfavor"; it is created `completed`, with no to-do and no XP (Q010), and its empty reflection shows as "Falta la reflexión". Dromos changes the state: "Empezar a leer" (`planning` → `active`, writes `started` and `habitica_id`) and "Terminar" (`active` → `completed`, writes `finished`). Dromos writes nothing else in a book note.
- **S5** — The screen: "seria la principal, con los que estoy leyendo en algo asi como una mesa, y atras los estantes, luego si le doy click a un estante me muestran los libros de ese estante y si ya le doy a alguno hay si me mostraria ese libro como tal, claramente todo adaptado a lo nuestro"; on the book card as a modal: "Me parece bien lo que dices". Three levels: the reading room (the desk with the books being read, the shelves behind; a fifth signpost plank), a shelf (its books, in the same tab, with a way back), the book card (an Obsidian `Modal` with the reflection read-only, the seal, and the buttons "Empezar a leer", "Terminar", "Abrir la nota"). The approved mockups and the adjustments are in `08-Visual-Design.md`, "Resultados (2026-11-06)".
- **S6** — Layers for later art: "si en un futuro se puede mejorara me gustaria que se pudiera hacer". Each scene is built in layers (background, drawing, panels); the art itself is not in M7.

Assumptions (to confirm before U4):
- **A1** — Habitica's create-task API accepts `checklist` on a to-do. **Confirmed** (L9).
- **A2** — If Habitica fails, the state does not change; "Terminar" does not complete the to-do in Habitica (proposed in Q028).
