# M8 El archivo

Objective: a room inside the Library, "El archivo", with the resources saved to read, watch or listen to, by kind and by state; a resource is sealed only once its note says what it left the user.
Branch: `feat/m8-archive` · Delivery: work-unit commits on the branch, merged into `main` and released (0.13.0) only with the user's go-ahead; this repository delivers by local merge, not pull requests · Checks: `npm test` (Vitest), `npm run lint`, `npm run build`, then the user's look in Obsidian on desktop and Android.

## Specs

- **S1** — The request. 2026-11-09: "podriamos quizas almenos hacer la planeacion del nuevo modulo de articulos para leer, o quizas podriamos incluso llevarlo mas alla y convertirlo en un modulo de recursos, donde de una vez pueda agregar paginas, videos, articulos, etc. Estariamos llevandolo mas alla, pero podriamos ir planeando desde ya". 2026-11-10: "si arranquemos con m8 de una vez mientras pruebo".
- **S2** — Where it lives (Q029, decided A): "creo que me voy a inclinar mas por el apartado en biblioteca". A doorway "El archivo" in the reading room opens the archive in the same tab, with "← Biblioteca" back; the signpost keeps five planks. Mockups: vault `02 - Projects/Dromos/ANEXOS/biblioteca-mockup-10-archivo.png` (light) and `-11-archivo-oscuro.png` (dark); prompt and adjustments in `08-Visual-Design.md` "El archivo (M8, propuesta)". Frames 1 (doorway), 4 (phone) and 5 (card) are still to come from the user.
- **S3** — Which resources enter and their states (ADR-018; Q030, Q031). A `type: resource` note outside `Templates/` with a `saved` date enters; one without `saved` does not. Pending: `saved` and no `finished`. "Falta lo que te dejó": `finished` and `## Lo que me dejó` empty or missing. Processed (sealed): `finished` and text under `## Lo que me dejó`, read from the metadata cache like a book's reflection. Text without `finished` is still pending. Q030: "si vamos cokn tu recomendado pienso que primero debemos hacer toda la funcionalidad y ya luego hacemos la migracion" — only new notes enter; the migration is Esdras's, after this module.
- **S4** — Pigeonholes by `sourceType` (Q032, "si me parecen bien"): `post` → Artículos, `paper` → Papers, `video` → Videos, `podcast` → Podcasts, `tool` → Páginas, `course` → Cursos. `reference` never enters. A `saved` note with another or no `sourceType` shows under "Todos" as "Sin tipo". The verb follows the kind: read (post, paper, tool, no kind), watched (video, course), listened (podcast). A pigeonhole shows for each kind with at least one item, plus "Todos"; its count is the items not yet processed; selecting it filters the list.
- **S5** — The archive screen (`04-Game-Design.md` "El archivo"): the room drawing with the pigeonholes; a summary "4 por leer · 2 por ver · 1 por escuchar · 1 falta procesar · 12 procesados" without the zero parts; sections "Por leer y ver" and "Falta lo que te dejó" (most recently saved first) and "Procesados" (most recently finished first, the first six, then "Ver más (N)"); each row: the kind's drawing and label, the state in words, the title, author · domain, "Guardado hace 3 días", "Me sirve para: …" when there is a `why`, and "Abrir ↗" when there is a `url`. Empty: "No hay nada pendiente. Pásale un enlace a Esdras para guardarlo." English and Spanish copy.
- **S6** — The card (Q034, Q033): a modal like the book card with the data, the dates, "Para qué me sirve", "Lo que me dejó" read-only, the seal; buttons "Abrir el enlace ↗", "Marcar como leído" (visto / escuchado by kind) while there is no `finished`, and always "Abrir la nota". "Marcar como leído" writes only `finished` (today, `YYYY-MM-DD`); Dromos writes nothing else in a resource note and creates nothing in Habitica, and a resource gives no XP.
- **S7** — Release 0.13.0 and its check on desktop and Android, only with the user's go-ahead.
