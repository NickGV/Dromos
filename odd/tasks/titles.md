# Titles earned by big feats

Objective: the character's title is earned by big, rare feats instead of being only free text, and the user chooses which earned title the plaque shows. Backlog item of the vault's `02-Phases.md` ("Recompensas por subir de nivel (títulos ...)": "los títulos quedan para más adelante, por hazañas grandes") and ADR-019 ("Una idea para más adelante: títulos raros por hazañas grandes"; "Un título por nivel: descartado por Nick").
Branch `feat/titles`. Delivery: decisions, then mockups, then work-unit commits, `--no-ff` merge into `main` and a release only with the user's go-ahead. Runner: Vitest (`npm test`), then `npm run lint` and `npm run build`.

## Specs

Claude proposed (L1): "Hoy "Corredor" es texto libre en `Character.md`, y la idea sería que se ganen con logros concretos: terminar tu primera campaña, leer 10 libros, una racha de 30 días. Los irías juntando y elegirías cuál mostrar en la placa." The user accepted (L1): "si dale porfavor".

- **S1 Titles by big feats, granted by Esdras.** Titles are earned by concrete, rare feats ("terminar tu primera campaña, leer 10 libros, una racha de 30 días"), never one per level (ADR-019: "por nivel serían muchos y muy fáciles"). Earned titles are collected. Esdras grants them (L3): "yop prefiero que sea esdras quien lo haga me suena mejor que sea el quien es mi guia el que decida, estaria genial esa parte". Dromos does not decide a feat; it reads what Esdras wrote (assumption: in a note of his in the Dromos folder, like `Shop.md`, ADR-019).
- **S2 The user chooses the title shown, in Dromos.** "Los irías juntando y elegirías cuál mostrar en la placa." Chosen in Dromos (L4: "1", to "Lo elegís en Dromos ... Tocás tu título en la placa y elegís entre los que ganaste. Para esto Dromos tendría que escribir `title` en `Character.md` ... Eso pide un ADR que lo autorice, solo con títulos ganados."): Dromos writes `title` in `Character.md`, only a title Esdras granted, after an ADR the user accepts.
- Constraint from the vault contract (04-Game-Design): "`name` y `title` son del usuario y de Esdras; el plugin los respeta." Any change to who writes `title` needs an ADR the user accepts.
- **S3 The contract, ADR-020** (accepted L6: "me parece bien asi"), as written in the vault's `03-Tech-Decisions.md`:
  - `<folder>/Titles.md`, `type: dromos-titles`, written only by Esdras: a table `| id | title | feat | granted | why |` (`id` unique and lowercase; `title` as read; `feat` one sentence; `granted` `YYYY-MM-DD`; `why` in his voice). Esdras adds rows and never deletes them. Dromos reads it, never writes it, and names and skips a row it cannot read, as with `Shop.md`.
  - "Corredor" is the first title, its row dated `game_start`.
  - On the plaque the title is a button that opens a card with the earned titles (title, feat, date, Esdras's why), today's marked. Choosing one writes that text to `title` in `Character.md`: the only value Dromos ever writes there, and only a title of `Titles.md`. The plaque keeps showing whatever `title` holds.
  - A title dated after the last one seen on this device is announced once: "Esdras te otorgó un título: …".
  - Nothing reaches Habitica.
- **S5 The chosen form, option A "La lista"** (L8: "vamonos con la lista si", boards `A.png`, `A-2.png`, `Boton.png` and `Aviso.png` in `../dromos-worktrees/titles-mockups/`):
  - Only the title of the title line is a button, named "<title>. Ver los títulos", with `aria-haspopup="dialog"`; the level stays text. Dashed umber underline, ochre on hover, a focus ring around the title only.
  - The card, "Los títulos de <name>": today's title large ("Tu título", "Otorgado el <fecha> · N títulos otorgados por Esdras"), then "Los que ganaste", one row per title, newest first: title, date, feat, Esdras's why in italic signed "— Esdras". Today's row has an ochre bar and "En tu placa" as text; the others a button "Usar este título", named "Usar este título: <title>". An unreadable row of `Titles.md` is named at the foot. With a single title, a note that titles come from big feats.
  - Choosing writes `title`, closes the card and returns the focus to the title button; if the write fails, the card stays open with an error line and the title is unchanged.
  - The notice: a line on the plaque with the wax seal, "Esdras te otorgó un título: <title>" and "Ver los títulos", shown once per device (the last grant date seen is kept on the device; opening the card also clears it), announced through the existing live region.
  - With `Titles.md` missing, empty or holding no readable row, the title stays plain text as today (assumption). A `title` that is not a granted title is still shown, with no row marked.
- **S4 Mockups first, then the release** with the user's go-ahead, checked on desktop and Android.
