Objective: Dromos reads the notes that Kairos (phone time) and Cheshbon (spending) write into Logos, first as a "your week" card, then as titles and streaks. Planned with the `esdras`, `kairos` and `cheshbon` sessions on 2026-11-17.

## Specs

The user's request (L1): "A base de crear notas directamente en logos se puede conseguir una integración casi full de los 3 sistemas sin ningún problema [...] la idea es que como ellos pueden copiar en archivos o crear archivos directamente en logos entonces podriamos realizar algo con esa data. [...] con el fin de alguna forma juntar todo el software que yo utilizo y tener lo que se necesita todo en un mismo lugar". Asked "¿querés que estos datos solo se muestren en Dromos, o también que cuenten para el juego?", the user asked "que piensas que seria mas genial?"; Claude recommended the game in two steps (first show, then titles and streaks) with the rule "Dromos premia, nunca castiga", and the user answered (L3): "si dale me parece bien".

- **S1 The week card (step 1).** A card on the home screen: "your week", with the phone from Kairos (minutes, and the apps or days over a limit) and the money from Cheshbon (spent, income, net). Assumption: both show the last complete ISO week, Monday to Sunday, because Cheshbon's default note is weekly and Kairos's days are settled by then. `screen_minutes` is labelled "minutes in watched apps" (Kairos: only the enabled watched apps, not total screen time).
- **S2 Late notes.** The card says when an app's latest note is late, so a silent stop like Kairos's after 2026-11-13 shows the next day ("Con la tarjeta lo verías al día siguiente"). Assumption: Kairos is late when its latest `date` is older than the day before yesterday (it writes yesterday every 6 h); Cheshbon when its latest weekly `end` is more than 8 days before today (the weekly note arrives on Monday, around 06:00 or a few hours later).
- **S3 Read only.** Dromos reads only frontmatter, by `type` (`kairos-day`, `cheshbon-spending-note`). It never creates, edits, renames, moves or deletes those notes and never adds keys to them (Kairos rewrites its whole frontmatter). A day or week without a note means "no data", never a failure. Copies such as "2026-11-13 (1).md" count once: one Kairos day per `date`, one Cheshbon note per `period`. It never shows merchants, accounts, `first_app` or written reasons. Assumption: no folder fallback and no backfill; the notes Kairos wrote before it adds `type` are not read, which only affects the first week. A Kairos day D is settled from D+2 (L6); before that it can still change, so the streaks of S4 count only settled days.
- **S4 Titles and streaks (step 2, later).** Only after the data has arrived reliably for about two weeks. Simple signals only. "Dromos premia, nunca castiga": no gold (Kairos's rule: the note is a report, not a ledger), no penalties. Which titles is an open decision for later. What the sessions found about the signals (L4):
  - Phone: a Kairos day with at least one limited app (`under_limit` or `exceeded` not empty) and `exceeded` empty. `exceeded` measures the free limit, so a day with bought minutes counts as over it; whether that is the right signal is the user's call.
  - Money: not `net > 0`, which follows the week the pay lands in. The candidate is the week's `spent` below the average of the last few weekly notes, built from the frozen keys. To decide with the user.
- **S5 Contracts.** An ADR in the vault (Esdras, amending ADR-013): `kairos-day` and `cheshbon-spending-note` in the dictionary, system family, root folders `Kairos/` and `Cheshbon/`, one writer per folder, no bare-name links. Kairos writes `type: kairos-day`. Cheshbon's 9 keys are frozen: `type`, `period`, `start`, `end`, `spent`, `income`, `net` (whole COP), `currency`, `generated_at`.
