Objective: the character gets a drawn portrait on the plaque, a ready-made wise man of the era, and Esdras gets his own in his letter, chosen by the user from mockups before any code.   - The plaque's portrait starts as the landscape's walker, a young runner with no knowledge yet, "buscando la grandesa", and changes as the character levels up, earns skills and titles; the walker in the landscape changes with it, so both are the same person.
  - Kept from option B, which the user liked ("me gusta como se ve", L6): the figure under an arch on the plaque with the level medallion on its corner, drawn in Dromos's own style (flat fills on theme tokens, umber hairlines, ochre accents), readable in light and dark themes and at phone width; calm motion at most, held still under reduced motion, offline and out of sight. The medallion, the name, the title, the XP bar, the radar and the skills keep their meaning and their screen-reader text; the portrait is decoration (assumption).
  - L9: "no de 5 en 5 niveles, sino mucho mas, incluso si se puede podria ser convinacion no solo de nivel si no de skills y demas", "pensando que me voy a demorar años en llegar a ese nivel, me refiero a 10 o mas años", "no deberia ser tan sencillo". A stage is reached by combined requirements (level, skills and their ranks, campaigns, books, days on the road), and the last one takes 10 or more years. About 12 stages (provisional thresholds in L9, pending the user's word on the curve).
  - Settled (L7, L8, L11): the stage sets the figure's age, clothes and gear; each earned skill adds one object (the 3 highest skills with an object, one per spot, the higher skill winning a shared spot); the title stays words, since it is free text in `Character.md` ("`name` y `title` son tuyos y de Esdras"). The level curve stays as it is (L11: "si me parece bien, cualquier cosa luego la podemos cambiar si es necesario").
  - The stage is the highest one whose requirements all hold (assumption: no memory of a stage once reached). Defaults below, changeable in `Rules.md` so they can be adjusted later ("cualquier cosa luego la podemos cambiar"). "Skills" are earned (unlocked) skills; "rank N" is the N-th rank of `RANK_LEVELS`; "campaigns" are finished campaigns; "books" are finished books; "days" are days since `game_start`.
